## EXPLORING THE G HUB INTERFACE LAYOUT

Let's take a look at the basic layout of the G HUB interface. In the upper left hand corner of the Home Page there is a navigation icon that will give you access to global G HUB settings, apps and games profiles and the community as well as some useful web links.

CUSTOMIZING FROM G HUB HOME PAGE

Your connected devices are in the center of the Home Page. Simply click on a device to access the features and settings for that device. To get started, click on the device you want to configure and dive into changing your DPI and remapping buttons for your favorite games, or just set your [LIGHTSYNC RGB](https://www.logitechg.com/en-us/software/guides/lightsync-rgb) color and get back to gaming!

![](https://resource.logitechg.com/w_776,h_437,ar_16:9,c_fill,q_auto,f_auto,dpr_1.0/d_transparent.gif/content/dam/gaming/en/ghub-update/g-hub-basics/ghub-basics-lp-highlight-3.jpg)

PROFILES AND DEVICE VIEWS

At the top of the Home Page are the user account settings and apps and games profile menu. You can also change the view of your devices from “Tile” to “List” view, which can be super helpful when you have a ton of devices in your gaming setup.

![](https://resource.logitechg.com/w_776,h_437,ar_16:9,c_fill,q_auto,f_auto,dpr_1.0/d_transparent.gif/content/dam/gaming/en/ghub-update/g-hub-basics/ghub-basics-lp-highlight-4.jpg)

CONFIGURE FEATURES AND PRESETS

The device configuration page features tabs for settings like Sensitivity, LIGHTSYNC, and Assignments. Use presets for easy setup or download unique effects and settings shared by gamers like you in the Logitech community.

![](https://resource.logitechg.com/w_776,h_437,ar_16:9,c_fill,q_auto,f_auto,dpr_1.0/d_transparent.gif/content/dam/gaming/en/ghub-update/g-hub-basics/ghub-basics-lp-highlight-5.jpg)

G HUB: PRESETS & DEVICE MANAGEMENT

Take advantage of community setups for advanced features like [BLUE VO!CE](https://www.logitechg.com/en-us/software/guides/blue-voice) filters and KEYCONTROL. Use the settings cog on the device page to adjust firmware, power, and other device options. To upload your own presets for games like Valorant and CSGO, log in or create a Logi ID via the profile icon in the G HUB window.
