Remote Ops

# Command drones everywhere, all at once, from anywhere.

[Get started today](https://www.skydio.com/software/remote-ops?chat=sales#chat-with-us)

Rapid response or scheduled flight. Hand activated or Dock launched. One drone or many at once. Skydio Remote Ops software lets you fly wherever you want, whenever you want, and however far you want.

## Remote Ops gives you full control from launch to landing.

### Fly where you need to, automatically.

Intelligent Fly to Point lets you send your drone to GPS coordinates, addresses, or teammate locations—by selecting a point on the map or through integration with your dispatch systems. The drone flies there autonomously, with pilot oversight.

**Fly your way**. When it comes to controlling your drones, Remote Ops gives you multiple options. Fly with a keyboard, mouse, controller, or a combination. Every operator can choose what works best for them.

![Operator controls with mouse, keyboard, phone, or gaming controller](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/mgxz50fq/production-v3-red/f51a1a36634f6c27cd23f09f0fa0d8bcf167f518-1204x800.png?w=3000&fit=max&auto=format)

### Get there fast, with safety built in.

Skydio Pathfinder automatically charts and flies the safest, most efficient route—accounting for terrain, obstacles, geofences, and airspace rules—so operators can focus on the mission, not the map.

Learn about Pathfinder

## Let your drones work for you. Not the other way around.

Drones stationed where the work happens become an autonomous workforce to conduct scheduled missions, capture data for real-time intel or detailed analysis post-flight.

#### One operator, multiple drones.

Multiply your productivity by giving one operator the ability to command your entire drone fleet across multiple sites from one Remote Ops command center.

Watch multi-drone in action

**Easily capture repetitive data sets** with powerful mission planning and scheduling tools. Skydio’s Visual Positioning System enables real-time environmental mapping for precise, reliable data collection—mission after mission.

![Create and save mission paths In Remote Ops](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/mgxz50fq/production-v3-red/738871bc6e3e98e20f67bb4ea32e6ed7618fdd0e-1920x1080.png?w=3000&fit=max&auto=format)

**Streamline asset inspection workflows** by uploading your existing datasets and turning them into ready-to-fly missions in just a few clicks. Skydio automates mission planning so your teams can focus on analysis, not flight planning.

![asset-based inspection interface with automated mission planning points](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/mgxz50fq/production-v3-red/9b923cb4817eef9c0e17306b8429c14ad34b0e82-3334x1910.png?w=3000&fit=max&auto=format)

### Autonomous tracking that frees the pilot.

**Skydio Shadow** locks onto subjects with precision most pilots can’t match, turning the Skydio X10 into a reliable teammate that never loses sight. It automatically keeps a safe distance, avoids obstacles, and quickly reacquires a target if the line of sight is broken—so pilots can stay focused on the mission, not the controls.

###### Always ready, day or night.

Operations don’t stop at dusk. Skydio Shadow supports both color and thermal cameras, so you can track and follow subjects in any lighting condition.

### Augmented reality overlays for instant context.

Skydio augmented reality overlays project street names, addresses, and mission markers directly onto the flight screen. Operators stay oriented at a glance without breaking focus, so they can coordinate faster, navigate with confidence, and make better decisions in the moment.

## Safely get wherever you need to be with advanced autonomy and onboard AI.

Trustworthy obstacle avoidance lets you fly free in any direction. Sideways, up, down and backwards—even when you can’t see your drone. So you can easily complete complex missions with total confidence.
