Tackling cheating has always been a leading priority of ours, and we’re proud of our work in keeping cheating levels at all-time lows — our player surveys have also seen record-low complaints about cheating! Today, our Anti-Cheat team is giving you an update on some more changes we’ve implemented to boost your performance, keep cheaters at bay, fact-checking what you might have seen on the internet, and we’re adding a quick clarification on cheating bans:

**Performance Boost and Crash Fixes**

We’re dedicated to minimizing the toll on player PCs. We’ve identified and fixed a performance issue causing stutters on certain PCs, in particular those with slower hardware. A bug causing CS2 to crash upon launch has also been tackled, which will make game interruptions less common and frustrating!

**Strengthening Security Measures**

If you’re on Windows 11, you may already know about TPM 2.0 and Secure Boot as they are part of the requirements to install the OS. These two features prevent many cheats by design, which has resulted in many cheaters staying on Windows 10 to avoid these requirements.

To further help, we’re making sure some suspicious accounts on Windows 10 meet these standards too. In particular, we may require TPM 2.0, Secure Boot and HVCI (Memory Integrity) to be enabled before you are allowed to log into your account with FACEIT Anti-Cheat. These features will only be applied to suspicious accounts that can comply with these checks; for those less familiar with such features, feel free to access our [support page!](https://support.faceit.com/hc/en-us/articles/12452232454940-Account-specific-security-requirements)

**Debunking Misinformation**

There’s been some misinformation floating around about our Anti-Cheat system, particularly on YouTube. Most commonly, we’re seeing videos claiming that we can’t detect Direct Memory Access (DMA) cheats: as a matter of fact, we’ve been on top of these cheats since 2018, and they’ve been the most common source of cheating bans in the past year. We’re continually updating our protection layer to detect and block DMA cheats, banning any player attempting to use such cheats.

Cheat developers and scammers often wrongly claim they’ve outsmarted our Anti-Cheat: there are many examples of cheats pretending to be undetectable despite this not being the case. As a consequence, we often receive emails and support tickets from concerned players who believe these cheats are real and undetected.

There are all sorts of techniques used to market so-called “undetectable cheats” and trick people into buying them, such as:

- Cheat gameplay footage that is not on FACEIT (with the video edited to make it look like FACEIT AC is running)
- Cheat gameplay footage running FACEIT AC while playing on a local server. The AC is inactive if you are not in an active FACEIT match, an important detail often overlooked by those tricked by this scam
- Fake comments and reviews from a scammer’s alt accounts, to attempt to legitimize the cheat

Successful scammers will then provide programs that do not work (and in some cases, malware), or a cheat they already know is detected, causing the user to be swiftly banned.

**Our position on Cheating**

We’d also like to provide an update on the recent sv\_cheats incident. We’re pleased to inform you that our team swiftly identified the issue and applied a fix as soon as possible. Within just 2 hours of the problem arising, we began rolling out the solution and taking action against those who abused the exploit.

We have issued a total of 340 bans in response to the incident, in accordance with our cheating policies. The consequences have been kept identical: Elo has been reverted in affected matches, and standard cheating bans of 2 years have been applied.

We wish to remind all players that the FACEIT platform is here to serve the competitive community by providing the best competitive experience possible. It is not the place to ‘test out’ cheats, or compromise game integrity. Using the console to enter a convar explicitly enabling cheats is unacceptable, and **our message is clear: cheating will never be tolerated on FACEIT**.

Keep grinding and play fair.
