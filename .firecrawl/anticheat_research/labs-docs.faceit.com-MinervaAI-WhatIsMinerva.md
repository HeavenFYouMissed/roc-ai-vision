# What is Minerva

## Overview [​](https://labs-docs.faceit.com/MinervaAI/WhatIsMinerva/\#overview "Direct link to heading")

Minerva is your one-stop-shop solution for toxic and abusive behaviors detection and prevention in online gaming communities.

The Minerva engine analyses text, audio and behavioral data of your players to detect toxicity and other abuses.

You can choose to implement the Minerva’s Open APIs separately and access one product (e.g. Minerva Open Text), or you can decide to opt into multiple products.

Not all the Minerva Open Products are available for General Audience yet. Please discuss your specific requirements with your Business Development contact.

## Available Products [​](https://labs-docs.faceit.com/MinervaAI/WhatIsMinerva/\#available-products "Direct link to heading")

These products are currently available for game developers:

- Minerva Open Text

The team is currently working on Minerva Open Voice and different behavioral detection algorithms which are currently being tested on the FACEIT platform and include the ability to detect:

- AFKing
- Blocking teammates
- Intentional friendly fire

Minerva Open Voice is in the works, while the behavioral models are due to be released to the General Audience in 2024.
